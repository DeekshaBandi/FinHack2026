# Allstate Corporation (ALL) - Climate Risk Profile

## Company Overview
- **Ticker**: ALL
- **Sector**: Financials
- **Industry**: Property & Casualty Insurance
- **Headquarters**: Northbrook, Illinois
- **Market Cap**: ~$48 billion
- **Annual Revenue**: ~$57 billion
- **Policies in Force**: 38.4 million (as of February 2026)

## Physical Climate Risk Assessment

### Overall Risk Level: VERY HIGH
As a property and casualty insurer, Allstate's business model is directly exposed to climate-driven catastrophe losses. The company pays claims when disasters strike, making it a **first-order climate risk bearer**.

### Catastrophe Loss Exposure:
Unlike other industries where climate events disrupt operations, insurers **pay out** when events occur:
- Hurricane → Allstate pays homeowner claims
- Wildfire → Allstate pays property claims
- Flood → Allstate pays flood insurance claims
- Hail/Wind → Allstate pays auto and property claims

## Hurricane Exposure

### Hurricane Risk Score: 85%
Allstate has significant exposure to hurricane-prone markets:
- **Florida**: Major homeowners market
- **Texas**: Auto and home exposure
- **Louisiana**: Coastal property exposure
- **Southeast Coast**: Georgia, Carolinas

### Hurricane Impact Mechanics:
1. Storm makes landfall
2. Policyholders file claims
3. Allstate pays out billions in claims
4. Stock price typically drops 5-15%
5. Reinsurance recoveries offset some losses
6. Premium increases follow in subsequent years

## Wildfire Exposure

### Wildfire Risk Score: 70%
California wildfire exposure is a critical concern:
- **California Market**: Major presence in fire-prone areas
- **Recent Actions**: Allstate has reduced California exposure
- **Regulatory Challenges**: State limits on rate increases

### Wildfire Loss Dynamics:
- Single fire can generate $500M+ in claims
- Claims concentrated in short time window
- Litigation and bad faith claims extend losses
- Reinsurance protection critical

## Flood Exposure

### Flood Risk Score: 78%
Flood exposure comes through multiple channels:
- **NFIP Policies**: Allstate writes flood insurance
- **Residual Coverage**: Some flood coverage in standard policies
- **Auto Flood**: Comprehensive auto covers flood damage
- **Business Interruption**: Commercial policies

## Climate Risk as Business Model Risk

### The Insurer's Dilemma:
1. **Pricing Challenge**: Must charge enough premium to cover losses
2. **Regulatory Limits**: States restrict rate increases
3. **Competition**: Raising prices loses customers
4. **Model Risk**: Climate change makes historical data less predictive
5. **Capital Requirements**: Must hold reserves for tail events

### Strategic Responses:
- **Rate Increases**: Pushing through higher premiums where allowed
- **Exposure Reduction**: Exiting highest-risk markets
- **Reinsurance**: Buying protection from global reinsurers
- **Technology**: Using AI/data for better risk selection
- **Diversification**: Growing lower-risk business lines

## Financial Impact Scenarios

### Category 5 Hurricane (Florida):
- **Gross Claims**: $3B - $8B depending on path
- **Reinsurance Recovery**: 50-70% of excess losses
- **Net Impact**: $1B - $3B
- **Stock Price Impact**: -15% to -25%
- **Rating Agency Action**: Possible outlook downgrade

### Major California Wildfire Season:
- **Gross Claims**: $1B - $3B
- **Regulatory/Legal**: Bad faith suits add costs
- **Net Impact**: $500M - $1.5B
- **Stock Price Impact**: -10% to -15%

### Combined Cat Year (Multiple Events):
- **Total Cat Losses**: Could exceed $5B
- **Capital Strain**: May need to raise equity
- **Stock Price Impact**: -25% to -35%
- **Dividend Risk**: Could be cut to preserve capital

## Transition Risk

### Transition Exposure: MODERATE
Insurers face indirect transition risks:
- **Auto Insurance**: Electric vehicles change risk profile
- **Liability**: Climate litigation could create new exposures
- **Investment Portfolio**: Fossil fuel investments may decline
- **Regulatory**: ESG disclosure requirements

## Supply Chain Contagion Risk

### As Claim Payer:
Allstate is at the **end** of the climate contagion chain:
- Climate event occurs
- Physical damage to insured properties
- Claims filed
- Allstate pays out

### Insurance Market Contagion:
- Major losses can destabilize insurance markets
- Reinsurance capacity can tighten
- Pricing hardens across the industry
- Some markets may become uninsurable

## Investment Implications

### For Long Positions:
- **High Climate Beta**: Stock moves inversely to climate disasters
- **Seasonal Pattern**: Underperforms during hurricane season
- **Recovery Potential**: Prices harden after events, improving future margins
- **Dividend Yield**: Attractive ~2.5% yield when stable

### For Short Positions:
- **Primary Climate Hedge**: Profits when disasters strike
- **Timing**: Enter before hurricane season (June-November)
- **California Wildfire Season**: Fall months add exposure
- **Correlation**: Moves opposite to physical risk companies

### Hedge Effectiveness:
Allstate is one of the **best direct hedges** against climate event risk:
- Stock drops when events occur (opposite of most holdings)
- Liquid options market for tactical hedging
- Clear cause-and-effect relationship
