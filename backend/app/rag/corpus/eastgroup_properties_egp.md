## Financial Impact Scenarios

### Category 4 Hurricane (Florida):
- **Physical Damage**: $50M - $150M (based on portfolio concentration)
- **Rental Income Loss**: 1-3 months for affected properties
- **Insurance Recovery**: 60-90 day lag expected
- **NAV Impact**: -3% to -8%

### Extended Florida Hurricane Season:
- **Multiple Storm Risk**: Portfolio could face sequential hits
- **Tenant Disruption**: Supply chain customers may relocate
- **Insurance Cost**: Premium increases likely

### Severe Texas Flooding:
- **Physical Damage**: $20M - $80M
- **Tenant Business Interruption**: 2-4 weeks
- **Rental Income**: Largely protected by tenant leases

## Supply Chain Contagion Risk

### Tenant Industry Exposure:
EastGroup's shallow-bay industrial properties serve:
- E-commerce fulfillment (Amazon, others)
- Third-party logistics providers
- Light manufacturing
- Distribution and warehousing

### Climate Event Cascades:
- Hurricane disrupts port operations → tenant supply chains disrupted
- Flooding closes transportation routes → delivery delays
- Extended outages → tenants may seek alternative facilities

## Investment Implications

### For Long Positions:
- High-quality industrial REIT with defensive characteristics
- Hurricane risk concentrated in 2-3 markets
- Strong historical track record through storms
- Consider reducing position size during peak hurricane season

### For Short Positions:
- Limited short thesis given moderate risk profile
- Better hurricane hedges available (insurers, refiners)
- May underperform briefly after major Florida hurricane

### Diversification Benefit:
- Lower correlation to energy sector climate exposure
- Provides portfolio balance in climate stress scenarios
- Tenant credit quality provides downside protection
