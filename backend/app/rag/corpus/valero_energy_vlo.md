# Valero Energy Corporation (VLO) - Climate Risk Profile

- **Ticker**: VLO
- **Sector**: Energy
- **Industry**: Oil & Gas Refining

- **Market Cap**: ~$48 billion

## Hurricane and Cyclone Exposure

### Climate Adaptation Priorities:
1. **Louisiana, United States** - Cyclone exposure (Highest Priority)
2. **Texas, United States** - Cyclone exposure (Second Priority)

### Gulf Coast Refinery Exposure:
Valero operates 15 petroleum refineries with a combined throughput capacity of approximately 3.2 million barrels per day. Key Gulf Coast facilities include:

- **Port Arthur Refinery (Texas)**: 435,000 barrels per day - directly in hurricane path
- **Texas City Refinery**: 260,000 barrels per day
- **Houston Refinery**: 205,000 barrels per day
- **Meraux Refinery (Louisiana)**: 135,000 barrels per day
- **St. Charles Refinery (Louisiana)**: 340,000 barrels per day

### Historical Hurricane Impact:
- Hurricane Harvey (2017): Multiple Gulf Coast refineries shut down, causing $500M+ in losses
- Hurricane Laura (2020): Port Arthur operations disrupted for weeks
- Hurricane Ida (2021): Louisiana facilities impacted, supply chain disruptions

## Flood Risk Assessment

### Flood Exposure Score: 72%
Gulf Coast refineries sit at sea level or below, making them highly vulnerable to:
- Storm surge flooding during hurricanes
- Riverine flooding from heavy rainfall
- Compound flooding from simultaneous events

### Flood Mitigation:
Valero has invested in flood barriers, elevated critical equipment, and emergency response protocols at Gulf Coast facilities.

## Transition Risk

### Carbon Transition Exposure: HIGH
As a major petroleum refiner, Valero faces significant transition risks:
- Carbon tax scenarios could add $2-4 billion in annual costs
- Declining demand for refined petroleum products
- Regulatory pressure on emissions

### Transition Strategy:
Valero is investing $6 billion in renewable diesel and sustainable aviation fuel production to diversify revenue streams and reduce carbon intensity.

## Financial Impact Scenarios

### Category 4 Hurricane (Gulf Coast):
- **Physical Damage**: $800M - $1.5B in facility repairs
- **Revenue Loss**: 30-90 days downtime = $2-6B revenue impact
- **EBITDA Compression**: 25-40% reduction
- **Equity Impact**: -15% to -25%

### Carbon Tax ($100/ton):
- **Annual Cost Increase**: $3.2B
- **Margin Compression**: 200-300 basis points
- **Valuation Impact**: -20% to -30% over 5 years

## Supply Chain Contagion Risk

Valero is a critical node in the energy supply chain:
- Supplies refined products to thousands of retail locations
- Provides feedstock to petrochemical manufacturers
- Exports to international markets

A major disruption at Valero can cascade to:
- Oilfield services companies (Halliburton, Schlumberger)
- Petrochemical producers (LyondellBasell, Dow)
- Transportation and logistics providers
- Downstream consumer goods companies
